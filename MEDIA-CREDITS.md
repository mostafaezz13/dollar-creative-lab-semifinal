# Color-study footage

**Tears of Steel** (2012), directed by Ian Hubert; copyright Blender Foundation / Blender Institute. Original project: https://mango.blender.org/ and https://studio.blender.org/films/tears-of-steel/ . Film licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Attribution is also displayed below the color study in the app and exported HTML.

The color study is an illustrative demo, not client work or original camera LOG. Adaptations: six-second excerpt at 02:58, muted audio, widescreen crop, H.264 conversion, and an interactive simulated flat/log-like treatment transitioning back to the released picture. No source resolution was upscaled.

Retrieved film mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiaotangii/tears-of-steel-dialogue-dataset/main/data/tears_raw.mp4 . SHA-256 of downloaded film: `29854cbfdf81722ce77e037e7c77eb21b20e40c77c2f7b4298e3287af28ac744`. Only the pre-existing CC BY film excerpt is used; the mirror's dialogue dataset, annotations and other data are not incorporated.
